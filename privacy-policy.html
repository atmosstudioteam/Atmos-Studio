Atmos Studio Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 30, 2026

Last updated: July 31, 2026

1. Scope of this Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how information is processed in products and services released, published, maintained, or operated under the Atmos Studio name.

This Policy applies, in particular, to:

Community Discord and Telegram channels
Minecraft Modpacks, data packs, resource packs, and configs
Ko-fi Subscriptions and access to current builds
Modrinth Public releases and project pages
CurseForge Public releases and distribution
Support Email, community channels, logs, and crash reports

This Policy also applies to future Atmos Studio Products where this Policy is referenced, unless a separate privacy policy applies to the relevant Product.

2. Operator and contact information

Atmos Studio is an independent development project. The operator of Atmos Studio is responsible for the processing described in this Policy.

Privacy questions and data requests: atmos.studio.team@gmail.com

3. General principles

Atmos Studio aims to process only the information reasonably necessary to operate its Products, respond to requests, provide support, maintain security, diagnose errors, and prevent abuse.

Atmos Studio does not:

  • sell personal information;
  • rent personal information to advertisers;
  • use personal information for personalized advertising;
  • request passwords or Discord account tokens;
  • intentionally collect complete payment-card details.

Third-party platforms may independently use analytics, cookies, advertising systems, or payment services under their own privacy policies.

4. Community platforms

When a User contacts Atmos Studio or participates in an official Discord or Telegram community, Atmos Studio may process information the User chooses to share, including:

  • username, display name, and public profile image;
  • messages sent directly to Atmos Studio;
  • support requests, feedback, and bug reports;
  • attachments and other voluntarily submitted content;
  • moderation and abuse-prevention information.

Public community messages remain subject to the rules and privacy practices of the platform on which they are posted.

Information submitted to Atmos Studio is processed only to answer the request, provide support, maintain security, moderate abuse, or diagnose a reported problem.

Atmos Studio does not request Discord or Telegram passwords, account tokens, login codes, or complete payment details.

5. Minecraft modpacks and related Products

Unless clearly stated otherwise in a Product-specific notice, Atmos Studio Minecraft modpacks, data packs, resource packs, scripts, and configuration packages do not directly transmit personal information to Atmos Studio.

Local saves, settings, game logs, launcher information, and installation details generally remain on the User's device.

Atmos Studio may receive this information when:

  • the User voluntarily submits a game log, crash report, screenshot, save, configuration, or another file;
  • the User contacts technical support and provides information about their device or software;
  • a Product uses an online Atmos Studio feature that is separately disclosed;
  • information is provided through a third-party platform selected by the User.

A modpack may contain third-party modifications, libraries, launchers, or services. Those components may independently connect to the internet, check for updates, download content, or process technical data.

Independent processing by third-party components is governed by the relevant developers' and providers' own policies.

6. Ko-fi subscriptions and restricted Builds

Atmos Studio may use Ko-fi to publish current, experimental, early-access, or otherwise restricted Builds of its Products.

Depending on Ko-fi's available functions and the User's activity, Atmos Studio may receive or view:

  • Ko-fi profile names and public account information;
  • platform or subscriber identifiers;
  • subscription tier and subscription status;
  • subscription start, renewal, and expiration information;
  • access-entitlement information;
  • transaction references that do not contain complete payment-card details;
  • comments, messages, files, or other information submitted by the User;
  • information required to provide support or verify access.

Payments are processed by Ko-fi and its payment providers. Atmos Studio does not intentionally receive or store complete card numbers, card-security codes, or online-banking credentials.

7. Modrinth and CurseForge

Atmos Studio may publish public or previously subscription-restricted Product versions through Modrinth, CurseForge, or similar distribution platforms.

Atmos Studio may receive information made available to project authors by those platforms, including:

  • download and project statistics;
  • comments, reviews, reports, and support messages;
  • public usernames and profile information;
  • moderation and security notifications;
  • creator-reward or payout information, where applicable.

Modrinth, CurseForge, Overwolf, and similar platforms independently control information collected through their websites, applications, accounts, analytics, advertising, and payment systems.

8. Websites and GitHub Pages

Atmos Studio pages may be hosted using GitHub Pages or another hosting provider.

The hosting provider may automatically process technical information required to deliver and protect the website, including:

  • IP address;
  • browser and device information;
  • operating system;
  • requested page and request time;
  • security, diagnostic, and server-log information.

Atmos Studio static pages do not use first-party targeted advertising, profiling, or custom analytics unless a page clearly states otherwise.

9. Support and communications

When a User contacts Atmos Studio by email, Discord, Telegram, Ko-fi, Modrinth, CurseForge, or another platform, Atmos Studio may process:

  • name, username, or alias;
  • email address or platform identifier;
  • Discord or Telegram account identifiers;
  • message content;
  • screenshots and attachments;
  • game logs and crash reports;
  • device, operating-system, launcher, and software information;
  • other information voluntarily provided by the User.

Users should not submit passwords, account tokens, private keys, payment credentials, or other secret information.

Before submitting logs or crash reports, Users should review them for personal folder paths, usernames, IP addresses, authentication tokens, and other information they do not wish to disclose.

10. Purposes and legal bases

Information may be processed to:

  • provide Product functions and downloads;
  • perform commands and User requests;
  • verify subscription or access status;
  • store User and server settings;
  • provide technical support;
  • diagnose and correct errors;
  • develop and improve Atmos Studio Products;
  • prevent spam, fraud, piracy, and abuse;
  • protect Users, Products, and infrastructure;
  • comply with legal obligations and respond to claims.

Depending on the circumstances, processing may be based on the performance of a requested service, steps taken at the User's request, legitimate interests, consent, or compliance with legal obligations.

11. Sharing and third-party services

Where necessary, information may be processed or made available to:

  • Discord;
  • Telegram;
  • Ko-fi and its payment providers;
  • GitHub and GitHub Pages;
  • Google when Gmail is used for communication;
  • Modrinth;
  • CurseForge and Overwolf;
  • hosting, database, infrastructure, security, and error-monitoring providers;
  • professional advisers and public authorities where legally required.

Such providers may independently control information collected through their own platforms.

12. International processing

Third-party platforms and infrastructure providers may process information outside the User's country, including outside the European Economic Area.

Where required by applicable law, international transfers must be supported by appropriate legal safeguards.

13. Data retention

Atmos Studio retains information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

Retention may depend on:

  • the type of Product or service;
  • whether a subscription, account, or integration remains active;
  • whether the information is needed for support;
  • security and abuse-prevention requirements;
  • legal, accounting, or dispute-resolution obligations.

Technical logs are generally retained only while needed for diagnostics and security. Support communications may be retained while the matter remains relevant and for a reasonable period afterward.

Information may be retained longer where required by law or necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims. Backups may retain information for a limited additional period until routinely overwritten or deleted.

14. Security

Atmos Studio uses reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, which may include access restrictions, permission controls, protected credentials, software updates, and deletion of information that is no longer required.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can guarantee absolute security.

15. User rights

Depending on applicable law, Users may have the right to:

  • receive information about data processing;
  • request access to personal information;
  • request correction of inaccurate information;
  • request deletion;
  • request restriction of processing;
  • object to certain processing;
  • receive portable information where applicable;
  • withdraw consent where processing relies on consent;
  • submit a complaint to a competent data-protection authority.

These rights may be subject to legal limitations and exceptions.

16. Data requests

To request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or information about personal data, contact:

atmos.studio.team@gmail.com

The request should identify:

  • the relevant Atmos Studio Product;
  • the relevant platform;
  • the User's platform identifier, where applicable;
  • the requested action;
  • information reasonably required to locate the data.

Atmos Studio may request reasonable verification that the requester controls the relevant account.

Users must not send passwords, account tokens, complete card numbers, or other secret credentials.

17. Children and minimum age

Atmos Studio Products are not intended for individuals below the minimum age required to lawfully use the relevant platform or service in their country.

Atmos Studio does not knowingly collect children's personal information in violation of applicable law. Information discovered to have been obtained unlawfully may be deleted.

18. Automated decision-making

Atmos Studio does not currently use personal information for automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects on Users.

19. Changes to this Policy

Atmos Studio may update this Policy when its Products, distribution model, infrastructure, legal obligations, or data practices change.

The current version will be published on this page. The вЂњLast updatedвЂќ date indicates when the latest changes were made.
